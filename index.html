Welcome to Anbalayam

More than a shelter. A place to belong.

Anbalayam is an old age charity home created with a simple belief: every elder deserves to be cared for, respected and surrounded by kindness. Located in Punniam Vayal, Avudayarkoil, our home strives to offer a peaceful and loving environment.

When we care for our elders, we care for the values, stories and love that shaped us.
