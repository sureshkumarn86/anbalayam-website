About Anbalayam

A home built on compassion.

Anbalayam is an old age charity home in Avudayarkoil, Pudukkottai District, founded and run by Mr. Maram Raja.

Our purpose

A peaceful place to live with dignity.

Anbalayam exists to provide elderly people with care, companionship and a sense of belonging. The aim is simple: to create a warm home where elders can live with dignity and feel valued.

Founded and run by Mr. Maram Raja, Anbalayam grows through compassion and the support of kind-hearted people.
