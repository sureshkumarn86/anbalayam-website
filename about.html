About Anbalayam

A home built on compassion.

Anbalayam is an old age charity home in Avudayarkoil, Pudukkottai District, founded and run by Mr. Maram Raja.

Our purpose

Anbalayam exists to provide elderly people with a safe, caring and respectful place to live. Beyond shelter, we want residents to experience companionship, warmth and the feeling of belonging to a family.

Founded by Mr. Maram Raja

With compassion at its heart, Mr. Maram Raja founded Anbalayam and continues to guide its work. The home grows through care, service and the support of kind-hearted people.

A home of care, dignity and love.
